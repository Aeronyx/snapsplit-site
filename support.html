Support

Help with projects, cuts, and saved clips.

How do I continue a project?

SnapSplit saves your project automatically as you work. On the Projects screen, tap its row to reopen the video with your split points and saved-clip status.

How do I add a cut?

Choose a video from Photos, tap or drag the timeline to the moment you want, then tap Add split. Use Fine tune or the frame buttons for a more precise cut.

Where are my clips?

Tap Save all clips to export every section to Photos in order. Allow SnapSplit to add videos to Photos when prompted, then find the clips in your Photos library. Open View individual clips to preview or save one section at a time.

How do I delete a project?

On the Projects screen, open the project's ⋯ menu, choose Delete project, and confirm. This removes its stored source-video copy, split points, and saved-clip status from the app. Clips already saved to Photos stay in Photos; delete those separately in the Photos app if you no longer want them. iOS device backups may retain older project copies under your backup settings.
