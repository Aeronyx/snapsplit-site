Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

SnapSplit splits a video you choose into clips on your iPhone. The app does not require an account.

Videos and Photos access

When you choose a video through Apple's Photos picker, SnapSplit receives that selected video and makes a temporary copy on your device. It uses the copy to play the video, show timeline thumbnails, and export the clips you request. The picker does not give SnapSplit access to your other photos or videos.

When you save a clip, SnapSplit requests permission to add videos to Photos. It uses that permission only to save the clips you request. Your saved clips remain in Photos and may sync through iCloud Photos if you have that enabled. SnapSplit does not post clips to Snapchat or any other service.

Data sent or shared

SnapSplit does not upload your videos, clips, or app usage to our servers. The app has no ads, tracking, analytics, or third-party SDKs, and it does not share your video content with other companies. Apple handles the Photos picker, Photos library, iCloud Photos, and TestFlight under Apple's own privacy practices.

If you use a TestFlight beta, the developer can see the email address used to invite you, your testing status, beta installation and session metrics, and crash reports through Apple. If you send feedback, the developer can also receive your comments, screenshots, and associated tester and device details. We use that information only to manage testing, fix problems, and improve SnapSplit; the app has no separate analytics service.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

SnapSplit deletes the imported working copy when you leave the editor and removes temporary exported files after saving or an export failure. If the app is interrupted, temporary files may remain until iOS clears them; uninstalling SnapSplit removes any remaining app temporary files. Clips saved to Photos remain there until you delete them in Photos.

You choose which video to open. You can revoke SnapSplit's permission to add to Photos in iPhone Settings at any time; this does not delete clips already saved. SnapSplit has no account or server-held videos to delete. You can stop TestFlight testing in TestFlight. We keep tester information and feedback available to us in App Store Connect while the beta is active and delete any copies we download after the related issue is resolved. Apple controls retention of its own TestFlight records. To ask us to remove you from testing or delete beta feedback in our control, contact SnapSplit support.
